# Rights and attribution

This is an independent visual research project about Ellsworth Kelly. It lets readers compare reported color, shape, chronology and scale, inspect conflicting sources, and see where evidence is missing. It is not a complete or authenticated catalogue raisonné. The artist's estate, Foundation, museums, galleries, publishers and scholars named here have not endorsed the project.

## Research and metadata

Factual records keep their source links. A listed collection can reflect an earlier publication or auction; unknowns and disagreements are preserved. Original editorial notes, classifications, reconciliation decisions and estimates are research contributions, not authentication or valuation advice.

The project’s original research contribution is licensed under CC BY 4.0 as specified in [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md). This applies only to rights the project can grant; no blanket data license is asserted over material supplied by other people. Facts, third-party text, artwork rights and database rights can have different legal treatment; attribution does not create permission. Creative Commons explains these limits in its [licensing guidance](https://creativecommons.org/faq/#may-i-apply-a-cc-license-to-my-work-if-it-incorporates-material-used-under-fair-use-or-another-exception-or-limitation-to-copyright).

## Artwork studies

The SVGs are approximate visual interpretations of Kelly's works. They simplify surfaces, contours and screen colors; some show a documented installation or projected view rather than a verified frontal outline. Recorded measurements and estimated display sizes remain separate.

**The SVGs and underlying artworks are excluded from the repository's MIT software license and from any blanket open-content license.** An editable vector is not a copyright-free artwork. The project has not established permission or an individual fair-use determination for every study. Public availability does not grant downstream permission to reproduce, sell, print, merchandise or otherwise reuse an artwork representation.

The intended critical and comparative use matters, but calling a project research, criticism or fair use does not settle its legal status. U.S. fair use requires a fact-specific assessment; other jurisdictions differ. See the [rights review](docs/RIGHTS-REVIEW.md) for the unresolved issues and publication options.

Downloaded source photographs, book scans, full-page OCR and private reference files are excluded from this repository. Their omission does not clear rights in the underlying artworks. For rights and reproductions, the [Ellsworth Kelly Foundation's official page](https://ellsworthkelly.org/foundation/) lists **rights@ekelly.org**. This is a rights-inquiry route, not evidence that permission has been granted.

For a correction or rights concern, open a repository issue identifying the record or asset and the concern. Do not include private information. A rights holder may request a private discussion before supplying supporting documents.
