# Publication rights review

Reviewed November 8, 2026. This is a documented publishing assessment based on the primary sources below, not a legal opinion or a promise of protection. No rights holder or scholar has been contacted, and no permissions are recorded by this review.

## Adopted licenses and remaining decision

The project has adopted **MIT for its original software** and **CC BY 4.0 for its original research contribution**, expressly limited to rights the project holds. [LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md) defines the scope and exclusions; [LICENSE](../LICENSE) contains the standard MIT terms. The SVG artwork representations, underlying artworks and third-party material are excluded from both grants. The mixed dataset and SVG directory are not blanket open-licensed.

That license split clarifies reuse; it does not itself make public display/distribution of the artwork studies lawful. If the publishing goal is to minimize unresolved artwork-rights exposure, publish code and curated factual metadata first, with image placeholders and source links, and obtain permission or qualified, use-specific legal review before releasing the study assets. If the owner elects to publish the visual research under a fair-use rationale instead, record that as an unresolved, informed publishing decision rather than a completed clearance. A custom domain, a disclaimer, lower resolution, absence of advertising or a takedown route does not guarantee fair use.

The existing public Git history may already contain SVG assets. Removing a download button or excluding assets from a new deployment does not remove copies from that history. Any rights-driven change needs a deliberate repository/history and deployment plan; this review does not perform one.

## The actual critical purpose

The strongest account of this project is concrete: it treats color, support shape, chronology and relative scale as questions for comparison. A reader can inspect all provisionally blue works, distinguish a painted white area from a gap between panels, compare repeated titles without assuming identity, and switch between recorded dimensions and explicitly estimated display sizes. Source disagreements and missing works remain visible.

These functions can support criticism and scholarship. They should be accompanied by specific observations and evidence, rather than a wall of decorative reproductions or a generic “for education” label. A title/color filter by itself does not settle the question. Nor should the software imply that a screen redrawing replaces the experience, surface, installation, conservation record or scholarly catalogue of a painting.

## U.S. fair-use factors: preliminary application

Section 107 names criticism and research as possible qualifying purposes, while requiring consideration of four factors. The following is an application to this project, not a finding that those factors have been satisfied. [17 U.S.C. §107](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html#107).

| Factor | Relevant project facts and unresolved questions |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose and character | Comparative analysis, citations, identity reconciliation and visible uncertainty support a research account. A freely reusable artwork library or merely decorative browsing serves a different use. Assess the website, individual exports and downstream distribution separately. |
| Nature of the work | Kelly's paintings are creative visual works. Reducing them to geometry does not turn their expressive compositions into factual metadata. |
| Amount and substantiality | A study often represents the whole composition; simple color and contour may carry much of its visual identity. Whole-work comparison may be necessary for a particular argument, but that necessity should be explained. |
| Market effect | The interactive analytical purpose differs from viewing an original painting. Yet downloadable, scalable vectors can supply artwork reproductions or adaptations and intersect with image licensing. No market or licensing-impact investigation has established that this risk is absent. |

The Copyright Office emphasizes that outcomes depend on circumstances, and provides no safe percentage or automatic educational exemption. [Fair-use guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/more-info.html).

In *Andy Warhol Foundation v. Goldsmith* (2023), the Supreme Court assessed the particular challenged licensing use. New meaning or an altered appearance alone did not resolve the first factor. This is a reason to analyze this project's actual display and downloadable-asset uses separately, not a claim that the decision decides this project. [Official opinion](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/22pdf/21-869_87ad.pdf).

## Why vector redraws need a separate boundary

The statute includes art reproductions within the definition of derivative works and reserves reproduction, adaptation and public-display rights subject to applicable limitations. A different file format does not by itself avoid those rights. Any copyright in newly added authorship does not supply rights in the underlying work. [17 U.S.C. §§101, 103, 106](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html); [Copyright Office Circular 14](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ14.pdf).

Do not assume that every geometric work is protected to the same extent, or that every simple shape/color combination is unprotected. That is an individual originality, scope and use question. Likewise, Kelly's death in 2015 does not establish that a particular work is public domain; publication history and applicable law require review.

The Foundation's site carries a Foundation copyright notice and explicitly lists **rights@ekelly.org** for rights and reproductions. No general open license or permission for this SVG collection was verified. Museum possession of a painting and access to an online photograph do not supply a project-wide reproduction grant. [Official Foundation page](https://ellsworthkelly.org/foundation/).

## License implementation

| Material | Proposed treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Original application, build and validation software | MIT; identify covered code paths and third-party exceptions outside the standard license text. |
| Original research prose, annotations and original database contribution | CC BY 4.0 has been adopted through LICENSING.md, expressly limited to project-owned rights; credit sources and mark excluded third-party content. |
| Bare factual metadata | Do not invent ownership over facts. Preserve provenance; avoid imposing attribution as though it creates rights in facts. |
| SVG artwork studies, underlying art, quoted descriptions and third-party images | Excluded from MIT and any blanket data grant; individual rights/status still unresolved unless a specific permission or other basis is documented. |

MIT permits broad software reuse subject to its notice requirements; it is not a license of someone else's artwork. [OSI MIT text](https://opensource.org/license/mit). CC BY permits broad use of rights validly licensed, with attribution and change notices, but excludes rights the licensor cannot grant. CC0 can waive only the rights its user holds; applying it to mixed third-party material would not clear that material. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); [Creative Commons FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/); [CC0 guidance](https://creativecommons.org/public-domain/).

A practical rights log should distinguish the study's source citation from its rights basis: record an asset ID, underlying work, rightsholder/agent if verified, permission or legal-review status, permitted media/territory/duration, required credit and date. Leave unknown entries unknown. Do not silently translate a museum's photo credit into permission for a vector adaptation.
