# Scholarly context and further reading

This atlas is a source-linked visual research tool. It is not an authenticated catalogue raisonné and does not replace close looking, museum records or the sustained scholarship below. These people are cited for their published work and verified institutional histories; none has reviewed or endorsed this project. Roles were checked November 8, 2026 and may change.

## Questions this interface can help examine

A color grouping can reveal recurrent relationships while hiding material differences: the same screen blue can stand for different pigments, lighting and photographic reproduction. A shape plot can compare a support's outline with a shape painted inside a rectangle, but only when those categories have been checked. A size comparison can expose a monumental work among apparently similar thumbnails; an estimated size must remain visibly distinct from a reported measurement.

These are research propositions, not conclusions about Kelly's intentions. Images, labels and source records should remain available as evidence against a proposed classification. Repeated titles do not prove that two sources describe the same object. An empty slot records the limit of this inventory, not absence from the artist's production.

## Reading routes

- **Yve-Alain Bois** — Professor Emeritus in Art History at the Institute for Advanced Study. His *Ellsworth Kelly: Catalogue Raisonné of Paintings, Reliefs, and Sculpture*, volume 1 (1940–1953, 2015) and volume 2 (1954–1958, 2021), provide the principal catalogue route for those years. Read his discussion of what a reasoned catalogue should explain, rather than treating catalogue numbers as sufficient interpretation. [IAS profile](https://www.ias.edu/scholars/bois), [IAS volume-two account](https://www.ias.edu/ideas/2021/bois-ellsworth-kelly-vol-2), [Getty interview](https://www.getty.edu/podcasts/art-and-ideas/yve-alain-bois-on-ellsworth-kelly/).
- **Jean-Pierre Criqui** — The Centre Pompidou records him as organizer of *Ellsworth Kelly. Fenêtres* (2019); a 2025 institutional event identifies him as a curator in the Musée national d'art moderne's contemporary collections. His account of the windows is especially relevant to distinguishing an observed architectural source from an artwork's constructed support. [Exhibition record](https://cataloguedesexpositions.centrepompidou.fr/Jean-Pierre_Criqui), [Kelly windows essay](https://www.centrepompidou.fr/fr/pompidou-plus/magazine/article/focus-sur-les-fenetres-dellsworth-kelly), [2025 role](https://amis.centrepompidou.fr/activities/2250).
- **Briony Fer** — UCL's newer emeriti directory identifies her as Emeritus Professor of History of Art; an older staff profile still says Professor. Her essay for *Ellsworth Kelly: Outside In* (2015) is a further route into the work. [UCL emeriti directory](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/social-historical-sciences/art-history/people/honorary-staff-and-emeritus-professors), [Smithsonian Libraries catalogue](https://collections.si.edu/search/results.htm?q=%22Fer%2C+Briony%22).
- **Ann Temkin** — Curator of *Ellsworth Kelly: Chatham Series* (2013) and organizer of MoMA's 2023 centennial presentation. MoMA currently lists her as Chief Curator of Painting and Sculpture and has announced a January 2027 transition to Strategic Advisor to the Director. The Chatham presentation is a useful route into related works viewed as a series. [MoMA biography](https://www.moma.org/about/senior-staff/ann-temkin), [centennial presentation](https://www.moma.org/calendar/exhibitions/5569), [announced transition](https://press.moma.org/news/ann-temkin/index.html).
- **Madeleine Grynsztejn** — Organized *Ellsworth Kelly in San Francisco* (2002) while SFMOMA's senior curator of painting and sculpture. MCA Chicago identifies her as its Pritzker Director and announced her planned departure at the end of 2026. Her Kelly exhibition and publication offer a collection-based perspective across media and decades. [SFMOMA exhibition account](https://www.sfmoma.org/press/release/sfmoma-presents-ellsworth-kelly-in-san-francisco/), [MCA transition announcement](https://mcachicago.org/pritzker-director-announcement/).

Cahiers d'Art's publisher page announces a third catalogue-raisonné volume for autumn 2026. This is a published schedule, not verification that volume 3 has appeared or was consulted here. Do not infer the project's coverage from that announcement. [Publisher's account](https://cahiersdart.com/fr/pages/cahiers-d-art-today).
